# Changelog

## v1.3.0 — 2026-11-16

- Pick the exact model year and model type — trim, GVWR and gas, diesel or EV powertrain — for every vehicle.
- Vehicles get generation-specific front ends plus lifelike details like rub strips, wheel wells, side markers, plates and mud flaps.
- Tap any part to see what it is, its measurements, its status, and buttons for View more and Move.
- View more opens a full breakdown of the part on your vehicle: photo, measurements, location, energy, cost, connections, pros and cons.
- Move shrinks the card into a compact editing bar so you can drag, nudge, rotate or switch sides with live fit checks.
- Parts can’t go through walls, wheel wells, the ceiling or each other — the app keeps placements physically realistic.
- Group parts into a cluster and tap Automate to get compact, weight-balanced or spread-out layouts for any area.
- Recolor any part, and swap finishes, variants and equipment from the same card.
- A green plus on every part adds it to your cart, your project and your profile.
- The new Compare tab puts parts or materials side by side with cost, weight, energy, insulation R-values and what each does to your build.
- The new Profile shows your parts, total cost, driving range and vehicle over a render of your build, with tiles that drill into each one.
- The new Tracker shows what you need to buy, what you have and what’s installed, with a next-step suggestion and 3D preview modes.
- Undo and redo, plus a cleaner mobile layout: bottom-bar sheets no longer repeat the same tab name at the top.

## v1.2.0 — 2026-11-16

- One-tap view modes — Exterior, Cutaway, X-Ray and Interior — make it easy to see inside the build.
- Cutaway mode automatically opens the wall facing you and lifts the roof as you orbit around the vehicle.
- Every catalog part now lists its real-world size in inches and centimeters.
- Selecting a part draws its length, width and height in 3D and shows its spec and as-placed size.
- Vehicles are rebuilt from 2025 published specs with accurate lengths, heights, wheelbases and interior vs. exterior widths.
- Each van has its own modern front end, wheel arches, roof profile and high-roof fairing.
- The box truck is now a 4x4 cab-over, the skoolie a Type A cutaway bus, and the pickup a 1-ton crew cab.
- A new part search finds the closest catalog module for brand names, models and specs like “100Ah lithium” or “Maxxair fan”.
- In the Android app, Search Online pulls live web results and pre-fills a part’s size, weight and price when it can.
- You can add any custom part with its own size, weight, cost, power and water specs, and place it anywhere in the build.
- The toolbar, catalog and part details have a cleaner, more compact layout.

## v1.1.0 — 2026-11-16

- NomadForge 3D now installs on Android as an APK, attached to every GitHub release.
- The Android app bundles its 3D engine, so it opens and works fully offline after install.
- Builds you save in the Android app stay on the phone between sessions.
- Check for Update inside the Android app opens the newest release in your phone’s browser so you can grab the latest APK.
- Settings now has an About section, this Latest Update list, and display toggles for the stats bar, grid and dimensions.

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-16

- Six base platforms — Sprinter, Transit, ProMaster, pickup camper, skoolie and 4x4 box truck — with wheelbase, roof-height and paint options.
- A modular catalog of 45+ off-grid components that snap into zones and swap automatically when a zone is already occupied.
- X-ray and hide toggles for the body, roof, cab and walls reveal the routed wiring and plumbing behind them.
- Tap any part to hide it, swap its variant, change its material or remove it from the build.
- An exploded-view slider pulls racks, solar, walls and cabinetry outward to show how the layers fit together.
- Five animated camera presets (hotkeys 1–5) open the rear or side doors and switch to a top-down floor plan.
- Procedural finishes for cabinetry, countertops, walls and flooring can be applied build-wide or per part.
- A live tracker shows itemized cost, payload against GVWR, axle and side-to-side balance, solar vs. draw autonomy, and water capacity.
- Engineering checks flag problems like an undersized inverter, a missing grey tank or an overweight build.
- A Phase 1–4 build timeline steps through insulation, electrical, cabinetry and exterior gear in order.
- Builds can be saved in the browser and moved between devices with JSON import/export, plus a CSV bill of materials.
