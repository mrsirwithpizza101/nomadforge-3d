# Changelog

## v1.0.0 — 2026-11-16

- Six base platforms — Sprinter, Transit, ProMaster, pickup camper, skoolie and 4x4 box truck — with wheelbase, roof-height and paint options.
- A modular catalog of 45+ off-grid components that snap into zones and swap automatically when a zone is already occupied.
- X-ray and hide toggles for the body, roof, cab and walls reveal the routed wiring and plumbing behind them.
- Tap any part to hide it, swap its variant, change its material or remove it from the build.
- An exploded-view slider pulls racks, solar, walls and cabinetry outward to show how the layers fit together.
- Five animated camera presets (hotkeys 1–5) open the rear or side doors and switch to a top-down floor plan.
- Procedural finishes for cabinetry, countertops, walls and flooring can be applied build-wide or per part.
- A live tracker shows itemized cost, payload against GVWR, axle and side-to-side balance, solar vs. draw autonomy, and water capacity.
- Engineering checks flag problems like an undersized inverter, a missing grey tank or an overweight build.
- A Phase 1–4 build timeline steps through insulation, electrical, cabinetry and exterior gear in order.
- Builds can be saved in the browser and moved between devices with JSON import/export, plus a CSV bill of materials.
